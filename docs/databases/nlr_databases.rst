NLR Comstock / Restock Databases
================================
ComStock and ResStock are databases maintained by the National Laboratory of the Rockies (NLR)
They contain detailed simulated building energy usage across the US for residential and commercial buildings
ComStock models US commercial buildings stock to estimate sub hourly energy consumption and load profiles.
ResStock models US residential housing stock. 

`NLR ComStock <https://comstock.nlr.gov/>`_

`NLR ResStock <https://resstock.nlr.gov/>`_

building-energy-profiles
-----------------------
The building-energy-profiles package is software maintained by the NLR that allows users to easily aggregate building
load profiles from both ComStock and ResStock. This package is utilized within the program in order to aggregate
scaled building profiles for buildings that are to be clustered. 

`building-energy-profiles <https://github.com/NatLabRockies/building-energy-profiles>`_
