Space Cross Validation (:py:mod:`nlr_ghe_screen.processing.space_cross_validation`)
==============================================================
These functions are responsible for utilizing the ESA WorldCover and HydroLakes databases to both cross validate
the land cover makeup of geometries and to gather information pertaining to lake depth and area.
